## Historical research

Published papers and catalogs behind the history told in the film.

### H1. Kravitz's 1967 cylinder-packing paper

<https://www.tandfonline.com/toc/umma20/40/2>

Sidney Kravitz, Packing Cylinders into Cylindrical Containers, Mathematics Magazine 40(2), 1967, pages 65–71, DOI 10.1080/0025570X.1967.11975768. The publisher issue page supplies the print year and article title. H5 discusses its role in the early literature. Use 1967 as historical context. The publisher's 2018 online date refers to digitization. Full original article access was unavailable during this revision.

### H2. Pirl's 1969 candidate and small-case work

<https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/mana.19690400110>

Udo Pirl, Der Mindestabstand von n in der Einheitskreisscheibe gelegenen Punkten, Mathematische Nachrichten 40, 1969, pages 111–124. The publisher metadata verifies the date. H7's author abstract records proofs through ten and conjectured configurations for eleven through nineteen. H5 reproduces the relevant candidate configurations and discusses the historical progression. The film uses 1969 as the conjecture baseline for fifteen and eighteen. Full original article access was unavailable during this revision.

### H3. Goldberg's 1971 candidate arrangements

<https://www.jstor.org/stable/i326664>

Michael Goldberg, Packing of 14, 16, 17 and 20 Circles in a Circle, Mathematics Magazine 44(3), May 1971, pages 134–139, DOI 10.1080/0025570X.1971.11976122. The journal issue identifies the title and print date. H5 section 2 records improvements for sixteen and seventeen and the twenty-circle construction. The seventeen candidate was later improved by Reis. Use 1971 for sixteen and twenty, yielding 55 years to the 2026 manuscripts. Full original article access was unavailable during this revision.

### H4. Reis's 1975 improved seventeen-circle packing

<https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/0025570X.1975.11976434>

George E. Reis, Dense Packing of Equal Circles Within a Circle, Mathematics Magazine 48(1), 1975, pages 33–37. The publisher record verifies its date. H5 section 2 attributes the improved seventeen-circle packing to this paper. Use 1975, yielding 51 years to the 2026 manuscripts. The publisher's 2018 online date refers to digitization. Full original article access was unavailable during this revision.

### H5. 1998 research paper, historical figures and search methods

<https://mathweb.ucsd.edu/~ronspubs/98_01_circles.pdf>

R. L. Graham, B. D. Lubachevsky, K. J. Nurmela and P. R. J. Östergård, Dense packings of congruent circles in a circle, Discrete Mathematics 181, 1998, pages 139–154. Full text inspected from the author's university-hosted scan. Section 2 at printed page 140 surveys the earlier candidates. Figures 2 and 3 at printed pages 142–143 label the relevant packings conjecturally optimal. Sections 3.1 and 3.2 describe repulsion-force optimization and growing-disk billiards simulation. The eighteen-circle discussion explains the multiple equal-radius arrangements obtainable from nineteen. Use real figure/page crops and clearly labeled illustrative simulations.

### H6. 2024 account of the established optimality results

<https://ijpam.uniud.it/online_issue/202451/10%20Ekanayake-LaFountain.pdf>

Dinesh B. Ekanayake and Douglas J. LaFountain, Tight partitions for packing circles in a circle, Italian Journal of Pure and Applied Mathematics 51, 2024, pages 115–136. The introduction on printed page 115 states that optimal radii had been proved for N at most 14 and N = 19. This paper provides another proof for fourteen. Use the dated 2024 statement to identify the five gaps below twenty-one at that time.

### H7. The 1999 proof for nineteen and its historical abstract

<https://link.springer.com/article/10.1023/A:1005091317243>

Ferenc Fodor, The Densest Packing of 19 Congruent Circles in a Circle, Geometriae Dedicata 74, January 1999, pages 139–145. The author's abstract records Pirl's 1969 proofs through ten and conjectured configurations for eleven through nineteen, the 1994 proof for eleven, and the present proof for nineteen. Together with H5's constructions, it supports the historical eighteen/nineteen comparison and the 1969 conjecture baseline.

### H8. Packing catalog with earlier candidate attributions

<https://erich-friedman.github.io/packing/cirincir/>

Erich Friedman's packing catalog credits the fifteen- and eighteen-circle constructions to Kravitz in 1967, sixteen and twenty to Goldberg in 1971, and seventeen to Reis in 1975. This earlier candidate attribution is documented in the production notes. The narrated age calculation consistently starts with the 1969 conjecture publication for fifteen and eighteen.
